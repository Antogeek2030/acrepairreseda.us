Air conditioners rarely fail without giving some advance notice. Recognizing the early signs of trouble lets you schedule service on your terms instead of waiting for a sudden, uncomfortable breakdown.

## 1. Weak or Warm Airflow

If the air coming from the vents feels weak or is no longer cool, several issues could be responsible: a dirty filter, failing blower motor, low refrigerant, or a problem with the compressor. Ignoring reduced performance usually leads to higher energy bills and greater wear on the system.

## 2. Unusual Noises

Buzzing, rattling, grinding, or screeching sounds are not normal. They can indicate loose parts, failing bearings, or electrical problems. Continuing to run a noisy unit risks further damage.

## 3. Frequent Cycling

Short cycling—turning on and off repeatedly in short intervals—puts extra stress on the compressor and often points to thermostat issues, improper sizing, or refrigerant problems. It also prevents the system from dehumidifying effectively.

## 4. Higher Than Usual Energy Bills

A sudden jump in electricity usage without a change in weather or habits often means the air conditioner is working harder than it should. Dirty coils, low refrigerant, or an aging compressor are common causes.

## 5. Water Leaks or Excess Humidity

Standing water around the indoor unit or persistently high indoor humidity suggests a clogged condensate drain, frozen coil, or refrigerant issue. Water damage and mold growth can follow if the problem is left unaddressed.

## What to Do Next

Turn the system off if you notice burning smells, excessive vibration, or water pooling. Then contact a qualified technician. Early intervention frequently restores comfort with a modest repair rather than a full system replacement.
